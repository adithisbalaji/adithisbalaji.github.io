Adithi Balaji

I am an undergraduate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, pursuing a B.S.P.H. in Health Policy and Management and a B.S. in Biochemistry. My academic and professional experiences have spanned molecular immunology, community nutrition research, rural mental healthcare policy, and federal legislation analysis. I currently conduct research at the Duke Human Vaccine Institute, where I perform computational analyses of HIV-1 Envelope structures to investigate mutation-associated conformational variation and its implications for antibody recognition. I am interested in exploring the potential causal relationship between infectious disease research and the development of evidence-based health policy interventions in the United States.
