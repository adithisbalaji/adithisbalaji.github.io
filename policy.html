Health Policy & Advocacy

My health policy expertise lies in examining the political and socioeconomic determinants of healthcare accessibility, particularly across rural and indigent populations. Previously, I contributed to the 2023 North Carolina Medicaid Expansion through the NCDHHS, coordinating outreach initiatives to address language, digital access, and informational barriers to health insurance enrollment. Partnering with senators in the NC General Assembly, I also explored the expansion of mobile health units as a means of extending medical and behavioral healthcare services to communities lacking nearby hospitals and medical facilities. With this foundation in health policy, I would like to investigate what causal relationship, if any, exists between Medicaid funded financial incentives for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) initiation and sustained PrEP adherence among the low-income, rural populace.
